The indexes written by each released pacs-rs image, for tests/upgrades.rs: <version>.sqlite
and <version>.postgres.sql (pg_dump, INSERT statements). Each holds the sample study of
docs/sample, sent to the release by scripts/upgrade-fixtures.sh, which made them.

They hold attributes of the sample study, adapted from LiTS: like docs/sample, they are not
under the licences of pacs-rs but shared under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/); see docs/sample-study.md.
